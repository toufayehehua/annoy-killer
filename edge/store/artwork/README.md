# Promotional artwork
The fictional travel page makes advertising cleanup visible without copying a real publisher's identity. Screenshots use the actual extension toolbar and particle renderer; a static pointer is added to the presentation page to show the interaction location. Particle timing differs slightly across captures.

English includes a marquee promotional tile (1400×560), before, particle and clean screenshots (1280×800), and a small promotional tile (440×280). Simplified Chinese includes the three screenshots and small promotional tile. Other localized store descriptions can inherit the English images until dedicated localized artwork is added.

The Alpine landscape is AI-generated (new generation, not an edit). Generation prompt: Cinematic photoreal Italian Dolomites at dawn, emerald alpine lake, peach-lit limestone peaks, wisps of mist, premium travel editorial composition, no UI, no text, no people, negative space on the left.
The particles themselves are screenshots of the shipped effect, not AI-generated particles.
