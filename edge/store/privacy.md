# Annoy Killer Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Annoy Killer (扰人杀手) lets you select and hide distracting webpage elements.

## Data processed on your device

The extension processes the current webpage structure to select, hide and restore elements. It stores hidden-element rules, the associated page addresses or site scopes, element identifiers or fingerprints, keyboard preferences and onboarding state in Microsoft Edge's local extension storage. Element fingerprints may include text from the selected element. Rule synchronization is enabled by default. When Microsoft Edge Sync is available and enabled for the signed-in Microsoft account, hidden-element rules, page addresses or site scopes, element identifiers or fingerprints (which may include selected text), and rule change/deletion metadata are synchronized through Microsoft Edge Sync across Edge devices. Keyboard preferences and onboarding state remain local. This data is not sent to the developer or to an analytics service.

When you hide an element, the extension may capture the visible tab temporarily to create its disappearance animation. Capture data is processed in memory on your device and is not uploaded or retained as a saved screenshot by the extension.

## Permissions

Website access allows the extension to select and hide elements and reapply your saved rules on pages you visit. Scripting initializes the selection interface when needed. Storage saves your rules and preferences and synchronizes rules through Microsoft Edge Sync. Alarms batch pending rule changes and retry failed synchronization. Context menus provide options, voluntary support and feedback links.

## No data collection or sale

The extension contains no analytics, advertising trackers, remote executable code or developer server connection. The developer does not collect, sell or share the webpage content, browsing history, screenshots or locally stored settings processed by the extension.

## Optional external links

If you choose to email feedback, the information you include is sent through your email provider to toufayehehua@icloud.com. The developer receives the information you voluntarily send to respond to your message. Please do not include sensitive webpage content unless necessary.

Voluntary donation links or QR codes open third-party payment services such as WeChat or Wise. Those services process payments under their own privacy policies. The extension does not access your payment credentials or process payments itself.

## Your controls

You can restore hidden items using the extension interface. Removing the extension removes its local extension storage. You can turn off rule synchronization in the extension options on each device. Turning it off preserves local rules and stops this device from reading or writing synchronized rules; it does not delete data already stored in Microsoft Edge Sync. Re-enabling synchronization merges local and synchronized rule changes.

## Limited Use

Annoy Killer uses website content and page addresses only to provide its user-facing element hiding, restoration, synchronization and animation features. This data is not sold, used for advertising, transferred for unrelated purposes, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
